## How it works

- `over` must produce a list. The body (`do`) runs once per item, with `{{item}}` and `{{index}}` available.
- `concurrency` runs that many items at once. Use it only when items don't depend on each other.
- `onError: skip` leaves failed items out of `results` and lists them in `failed`, with `failed_count`, instead of failing the plan.

## Limits

- The body can contain `agent`, `ask`, `filter`, `route` and `exit`, but not another `map` or `reduce`. Call a plan for nested iteration.
- `ask` steps in the body run one item at a time.

## Input

<ParamField body="concurrency" type="integer">
  Maximum items in flight; 1 (default) runs items one at a time.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="do" type="object | object[]" required>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="onError" type="enum<string>">
  fail (default): a failing item fails the step. skip: failed items are left out of results and listed in failed.

  Available options: `fail`, `skip`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="over" type="any" required>
  The list to iterate — usually a template like &#123;&#123;E0.issues&#125;&#125; that resolves to an array.
</ParamField>

## Result

Later steps read these as `{{<step id>.<field>}}`.

<ResponseField name="count" type="integer">
  How many items ran.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="failed" type="object[]">
  With `onError: skip`: the items that failed.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="failed.[].error" type="string">
  Why it failed.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="failed.[].index" type="integer">
  The failed item's position in the list.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="failed.[].item" type="object">
  The failed item.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="failed_count" type="integer">
  With `onError: skip`: how many items failed.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="results" type="object[]">
  Each item's result, in input order.
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```yaml Plan step

  tool_name: map
  input:

    do: "{{input.do}}"
```
